# Islamic Lecture Scholarly Transcription & Alignment Skill (Tafreegh)

## Master Production Architecture: Blind Literalism

Process automated raw draft transcripts (`<draft_transcript>`) and align them with vowelized canonical texts (`<matn_source>`) under **Blind Literalism** to produce Microsoft Word deliverables (`.docx`).

---

## 4-Folder Data Architecture

1. `01_Matn_Sources`: Canonical vowelized Markdown source texts (**Matn Source**) for **Blind Literalism**.
2. `02_Raw_Inputs`: Unedited draft transcripts from Gemini Notebook.
3. `03_AI_Outputs`: Archived `.docx` and `_AI.md` baselines generated by the export pipeline.
4. `04_Training_Data`: User-reviewed `.md` transcripts extracted from OneDrive (تم التسليم) for the Diff Loop.

---

## Input Modes

1. **Primary Mode**: Receives both `<draft_transcript>` and `<matn_source>`, aligning read passages via the **Sequential Cursor** under **Blind Literalism**.
2. **Dynamic Fallback Interleaving**: Receives `<draft_transcript>` and an incomplete `<matn_source>`, matching available passages in **Primary Mode** and switching per segment to **Fallback Mode** for missing book passages.
3. **Fallback Mode**: Receives `<draft_transcript>` alone when `<matn_source>` is absent, detecting reading boundaries via transition cues and linguistic shift.

---

## Section 1: Content Rules & Blind Literalism Protocol

1. **Verbatim Textual Mirroring**:
   - Transcribe spoken words in `<draft_transcript>` with a strict one-to-one correspondence, preserving Egyptian colloquialisms, spoken grammar, and the speaker's natural phrasing.
2. **Retention of Repetitive Loops & Audio Cues**:
   - Preserve automated transcription loops, spontaneous jokes, whispers, and deliberate repetitions verbatim as uttered.
   - Standardize laughter to: ههههه.
   - Strip only isolated vocal hesitation sounds: (آآ، ا، أ).
3. **Retention of Affirmative Fillers**:
   - Retain affirmative particles (نعم، صحيح، أيوة، اه) inline as part of the Sheikh's continuous verbal flow.
4. **Standardization of Salawat**:
   - Standardize verbal honors such as عليه الصلاة والسلام to: صلى الله عليه وسلم.
5. **Course-Specific Linguistic Context**:
   - **فقه البيوع (سعد الخثلان)** (Lecturer: Sheikh Osama): The Sheikh lectures primarily in Classical Arabic (فصحى) with occasional Egyptian dialect idioms. Transcribe spoken Classical Arabic verbatim as uttered with natural spoken endings.

## Section 2: Matn Processing Protocols

### Primary Mode (Canonical Alignment via Sequential Cursor)

Execute when `<matn_source>` is provided:

1. **Sequential Cursor Tracking**:
   - Track reading position in `<matn_source>` with a **Sequential Cursor** (`matn_matcher.py`):
     - **Forward Search Window**: Search `cursor -> cursor + 2000 chars` to match advancing passages and skips.
     - **Full-Range Look-Behind**: On a forward miss, search `0 -> cursor` to capture sentence-by-sentence re-reads of a previously read paragraph while preserving forward cursor advancement.
2. **Absolute Matn Authority**:
   - Copy matched text from `<matn_source>` character-for-character, preserving all diacritics (tashkeel), orthography (rasm), internal punctuation, and footnote markers.
3. **Spoken Boundary Clamping**:
   - Extract exclusively the Matn words pronounced by the Sheikh in `<draft_transcript>`.
4. **Interleaving Structure & Matn Re-reading Logic**:
   - **First Appearance**: Isolate standalone Matn on its own line as **(نص المتن المشكول)** separated by a single newline (`\n`) before and after so Word renders each paragraph break cleanly.
   - **Re-reading & Explanation (The "Last Bolded Matn" Rule)**: Compare repeated Matn words against the single most recent **(matn)** block (jumps to earlier blocks reset as First Appearance):
     - **Inline Citation**: When the repeated words already appeared in the last bolded Matn block, enclose them inline in regular-weight parentheses (الكلمة) inside the explanation paragraph.
     - **Isolated New Matn**: When the phrase includes Matn words beyond the last bolded Matn block:
       - **≤ 5 Old Words**: Merge old and new words into one standalone bold block **(القديم والجديد)** on a new line.
       - **> 5 Old Words**: Place the old words inline at the end of the current paragraph (الكلمات القديمة), break with a single newline (`\n`), and isolate the new words on the next line as **(الكلمات الجديدة)**. When the Sheikh reads both continuously with zero intervening explanation, merge them into a single standalone block **(القديم والجديد)**.
   - **Large Block Recounts**: When the last bolded Matn block exceeds 2 sentences, isolate the Sheikh's first extracted sub-clause on its own bold line **(الكلمات)** so it becomes the new reference anchor for subsequent inline citations.
   - **Inline Re-read Verses**: Format previously bolded verses or hemistichs re-read inside commentary as regular-weight inline citations (الشطر الأول، الشطر الثاني), joining hemistichs with a single space or an Arabic comma (،).
   - **Splitting Interrupted Matn/Sharh Clauses**: When the Sheikh interrupts a Matn or Sharh sentence mid-clause with commentary or a correction, split at the interruption: isolate each spoken Matn/Sharh segment on its own bold line **(...)** and place the Sheikh's commentary on regular unbolded paragraphs between them.

### Dynamic Fallback Interleaving (Incomplete Matn Sources)

Execute when `<matn_source>` is provided and a spoken book segment misses both forward and look-behind searches:

1. **Per-Segment Switching**:
   - Switch dynamically between **Primary Mode** (vowelized `<matn_source>` text) and **Fallback Mode** (unvowelized book text). Detect missing Sharh/Matn segments directly from `<draft_transcript>` and `<matn_source>` using transition cues (قال، قالوا، فإن قلت، قلت، وأيضا، فصل في، قال الشاعر), a 100% Classical Arabic linguistic fingerprint, and sandwiched context (reserving `04_Training_Data` strictly for the Section 7 Diff Loop).
2. **Context-Dependent Conservative Doubt**:
   - **Sandwiched Segments (Relaxed Doubt)**: When an unmatched Classical Arabic segment is bounded before and after by verified **Primary Mode** matches, classify it as a book omission and isolate it as Matn.
   - **Isolated Segments (Strict Doubt)**: When an unmatched segment lies outside sandwiched context, require an explicit transition cue and a pure Classical Arabic linguistic fingerprint before isolating it as Matn; keep unverified classical phrases inside explanation prose.
3. **Fallback Marker (`<!-- fallback -->`)**:
   - Prepend `<!-- fallback -->` above the standalone unvowelized Matn block in `.md` output (preserve canonical tashkeel only inside inner parenthesized Quranic citations):
     `<!-- fallback -->`
     **(نص المتن المستكشف بدون تشكيل)**
4. **Hybrid Summary Report**:
   - Emit the summary section تقرير المتن الهجين at the end of the Markdown file recording total segments, primary matches, re-reads, and fallback counts (`export_docx.py` strips this summary from the exported `.docx`).

### Fallback Mode (Single-Input Heuristic Detection)

Execute when `<matn_source>` is absent:

1. **Transition Cues (Triggers)**:
   - Detect reading boundaries after verbal cues: قال، يقول، بيقول، روى، جاء في الكتاب، قال المصنف، قالوا، فإن قلت، قلت، وأيضا، فصل في، قال الشاعر.
2. **Linguistic Fingerprint**:
   - Isolate 100% Classical Arabic (فصحى) book readings as **(نص المتن)** in plain orthography (preserving tashkeel only inside inner parenthesized Quranic citations). Close the Matn block at the first Egyptian colloquial marker.
3. **Conservative Doubt**:
   - Keep ambiguous classical phrases inside regular explanation prose or standard Quran/Hadith punctuation unless book reading is unambiguous.

## Section 3: Dialogue & Speaker Attribution

1. **Default Speaker (Sheikh)**:
   - Write the Sheikh's explanation directly in body paragraphs with zero speaker prefix.
2. **Student Turn Isolation & Styling**:
   - Isolate every audible student question, answer, objection, or interjection on its own paragraph prefixed with طالب: (followed by a single space) in regular weight, ending with an Arabic question mark (؟) or period (.).
   - Reserve bold styling exclusively for standalone Matn blocks **(نص المتن المشكول)**.
   - **Distinction Criteria**:
     1. _Direct Responses_: Answers to questions directed to the room (e.g., Sheikh asks: شهر كام؟ -> Student on new line: طالب: شهر رمضان.).
     2. _Live Objections & Comments_: Counter-arguments or comments initiated by attendees (e.g., طالب: بس كان فيه مشركين بيحجوا.).
     3. _Direct Address Responses_: Replies when addressed as يا فلان.
     4. _Sheikh's Rhetorical Devices_: Keep self-answered pedagogical questions (هل هذا واجب؟ لا ليس بواجب), hypothetical debates (تقول لي وأقول لك), and restated questions (أخونا بيسأل عن كذا) continuous within the Sheikh's paragraph.
3. **Inaudible or Muffled Student Speech**:
   - **Complete Silence**: Format total inaudible student turns without parentheses:
     طالب: صوت غير مسموع.
   - **Partial Speech**: Enclose the inaudible portion in parentheses after the captured words:
     طالب: كلمة (صوت غير مسموع).
   - **Proactive Student Insertion (Known Cue Rule)**:
     When a **Known Cue** from the list below closes a thought before a topic shift or response, insert طالب: صوت غير مسموع. on its own line between the Sheikh's cue sentence and his continuation. Treat mid-paragraph cues where the Sheikh continues immediately as rhetorical flow within the same paragraph.
     - **Known Cue List** (refined via Diff Loop):
       1. نعم؟ (prompting a student to continue/respond)
       2. بتقول إيه؟ (asking what someone said)
       3. يا فلان followed by a pause (addressing a student)
       4. صعب ولا سهل؟ (asking for student feedback)
       5. بتقول حاجة؟ (asking if a student spoke)
       6. إيه رأيك؟ (asking for a student's opinion)
       7. أنت بتجيب دليل آخر؟ (responding to student evidence)
       8. الحكاية؟ (prompting for confirmation)
       9. إيه السؤال طيب؟ اللي عارف السؤال ياخد توفي. (quizzing students)
       10. حد بيسأل حاجة؟ (asking if anyone has a question)
     - **Rhetorical Suffix Filtering**:
       Treat sentences ending with a **Rhetorical Suffix** (ها؟ or إيه؟) as pedagogical engagement within the Sheikh's speech; `diff_cues.py` filters these suffixes out of **Known Cue** nomination.
     - **Conservative Attribution**:
       Insert proactive inaudible student turns exclusively after a matched **Known Cue** at a thought boundary; otherwise keep speech continuous in the Sheikh's paragraph so human review can mark new cues for the Diff Loop.
4. **Attendee Name Anonymization**:
   - Replace personal attendee names in direct address (e.g., يا أحمد) with: يا فلان.
5. **Interruption During Matn Reading**:
   - When a student interrupts an active Matn reading, close the Matn block `)**`, place طالب: (نص المداخلة)؟ on the next line, and resume the Matn block on the following line **(تكملة المتن)**.
6. **Audible Scope**:
   - Transcribe human speech exclusively (Sheikh commentary, Matn reading, and student interventions), omitting non-verbal room or recording noise.

## Section 4: Header, Formatting & Microsoft Word Compatibility

Apply [Formatting & Microsoft Word Rules](references/formatting-rules.md) as the single source of truth for bracket hugging (§ 1), Word bold/spacing/ellipsis/punctuation rules (§ 2), paragraph-terminal periods and Arabic orthography (§ 3), Quran/Hadith enclosures (§ 4), header architecture (§ 5), and dialogue formatting (§ 6):

1. **Lecture Identity & 3-Line Centered Header**:
   - Resolve `LectureIdentity` (date `YYYY-MM-DD` or **Numbered Lecture Stem** `[N]-[subject]_[N]` / المحاضرة: رقم, plus subject keyword mapped via the Routing Table in `CONTEXT.md`).
   - Emit the 3-line centered header at P0–P2 and start P3 cleanly with the Sheikh's opening praise, stripping raw filenames (`.mp3`) and NotebookLM tags (`Source guide`, `button_magic`, دليل المصدر):
     بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم
     المادة: اسم المادة المعتمد بالكامل
     2026-10-17 (أو المحاضرة: رقم المحاضرة)
2. **Word Base Styling (`Word base.docx`)**:
   - 0.5-inch margins on all 4 sides, `Traditional Arabic` 18pt, inherited template text color, RTL paragraph/run direction (`w:bidi`, `w:rtl`), and bold runs (`w:b`, `w:bCs`) restricted exclusively to standalone Matn blocks **(المتن)**.
3. **Quran, Hadith, Poetry & Numbers**:
   - **Quran**: Enclose all Quranic citations (full verses, partial clauses, or single words) in regular-weight parentheses (آية) with full Uthmani tashkeel.
   - **Hadith**: Enclose verbatim or near-verbatim Prophetic statements in quotation marks "حديث"; keep paraphrased references unquoted.
   - **Poetry**: Isolate standalone verses with a spaced ellipsis between hemistichs: الشطر الأول `...` الشطر الثاني or **(الشطر الأول `...` الشطر الثاني)** per [references/formatting-rules.md](references/formatting-rules.md) (§ 2). Format inline re-reads per Section 2 (Inline Re-read Verses).
   - **Round Thousands**: Write round thousands textually: 30 ألف، 5 آلاف، ألفين، ألف.

## Section 6: Execution Pipeline, Post-Export Verification & Active Learning Prompt

Execute these steps in order to complete a transcription run:

1. **Concluding Du'a**:
   - End the final lecture paragraph with the canonical Prophetic council expiation du'a and a terminal period (`.`):
     والحمد لله رب العالمين وصل اللهم على نبينا محمد وعلى آله وصحبه أجمعين، سبحانك اللهم وبحمدك نشهد أن لا إله إلا أنت نستغفرك ونتوب إليك.
2. **Automated DOCX Export & Baseline Archival**:
   - Run `export_docx.py` on the generated Markdown file:
     `py "C:\Users\L\Documents\Tafreegh_Project\.agents\skills\tafreegh\scripts\export_docx.py" "<path_to_markdown_file>"`
   - **Completion Criterion**: `export_docx.py` exits with code `0`, writes the `.docx` to the OneDrive course folder, archives `_AI.md` into `03_AI_Outputs/`, and moves raw/matn inputs into `processed/`.
3. **Post-Export Subagent Verification & Fix Loop**:
   - Immediately after `export_docx.py` completes, dispatch a verification subagent (`invoke_subagent` with `TypeName: "self"`, `Role: "DOCX QA Validator & Fixer"`).
   - **Subagent Steps**:
     1. Run `verify_docx.py` against the exported OneDrive `.docx`, archived raw transcript, and archived `<matn_source>`:
        `py "C:\Users\L\Documents\Tafreegh_Project\.agents\skills\tafreegh\scripts\verify_docx.py" "<onedrive_docx_path>" --raw "<raw_md_path>" --matn "<matn_md_path>"`
     2. When `verify_docx.py` reports any errors (exit code `1`), fix every reported violation in `03_AI_Outputs/<stem>_AI.md`.
     3. Re-export in place using `--overwrite` to update the target OneDrive `.docx` directly:
        `py "C:\Users\L\Documents\Tafreegh_Project\.agents\skills\tafreegh\scripts\export_docx.py" --overwrite "<project_ai_md_path>"`
     4. Re-run `verify_docx.py` and repeat steps 2–3 (up to 3 iterations) until `verify_docx.py` exits with code `0` (`PASSED`, `Errors: 0`). If any issue remains after 3 iterations, halt and return the remaining `ValidationReport` issues to the user.
     5. **Completion Criterion**: `verify_docx.py` exits with code `0` (`=== Tafreegh DOCX Verification Report [PASSED] ===`, `Errors: 0`), and the subagent returns a structured verification report to the main conversation listing: (a) initial issues caught, (b) fixes applied to `_AI.md`, and (c) final `PASSED` report metrics (`Paragraphs`, `Matn`, `Student`, `Errors: 0`).
4. **Final Agent Response**:
   - After the verification subagent confirms `0` errors (`PASSED`), report the exported paths and verification summary using this response block:
     > "تم إعداد التفريغ وتصدير ملف Word (.docx) والتحقق منه بنجاح (0 أخطاء):
     >
     > - نسخة OneDrive: [مسار نسخة OneDrive المطبوع من السكربت]
     > - مسودة الذكاء الاصطناعي (.md): [مسار نسخة المشروع المطبوع من السكربت]
     >   بانتظار استلام الملف النهائي من مجلد (تم التسليم) لتدريب المهارة وتحديث قواعدها تلقائياً."

## Section 7: Continuous Learning & Auto-Improvement (Word Diff Loop)

1. **Ingestion & Dataset Archiving**:
   - Run `fetch_training_data.py` to fetch user-reviewed `.docx` files from OneDrive (تم التسليم), extract plain-text Markdown via `read_docx.py`, and save them into `04_Training_Data/`.
2. **Diff Analysis & Classification**:
   - **Matn Classification (`diff_inline_matn.py`)**:
     - `fallback_correction` (**Fallback Correction**): User revisions to segments marked with `<!-- fallback -->`, isolated from copy errors to preserve training signal fidelity.
     - `oral_citation` (متن شفهي عارض / نقل مستقل): User-bolded `**(...)**` segments around oral citations or Hadiths outside `<matn_source>`.
     - `matn_copy_error`: Alignment or boundary discrepancies against `<matn_source>`.
   - **Student Cues Detection (`diff_cues.py`)**:
     - Extracts preceding Sheikh statements before user-inserted طالب: صوت غير مسموع. as **Known Cue** candidates while filtering out **Rhetorical Suffix** endings (ها؟, إيه؟).
   - **Rule & Benchmark Updates**:
     - Update [references/dialect-rules.md](references/dialect-rules.md) with newly discovered colloquial normalizations and [references/formatting-rules.md](references/formatting-rules.md) with formatting refinements.
     - **Primary Mode Learning**: Save verified triples (`<matn_source>`, `<draft_transcript>`, `<final_result>`) into `examples/example-0X.md`.
     - **Fallback Mode Learning**: Append newly discovered reading triggers to Section 2, save fallback benchmarks into `examples/example-fallback-0X.md`, and keep user-added Word diacritics scoped to that single document.
3. **Completion Criterion**:
   - Report an itemized summary of newly learned dialect entries, discovered reading triggers, **Known Cue** candidates, and saved golden benchmarks to the user.
